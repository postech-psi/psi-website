The PSLV programme

The work behind
a few seconds
of flight

PSLV stands for POSTECH Science Launch Vehicle. Since its first recorded launch in November 2024, the programme has connected structures, flight electronics, propulsion and recovery in a student-built sounding rocket.

In December 2025, the public PSLV-II record reports a completed parachute mission and 360-degree onboard filming. The flight is one moment in a longer cycle of building, testing and learning.
